What's still ours

Websites used to have a robots.txt telling machines where not to go. Now it's the other way round. Every site has a humans.txt, listing the places agents agree not to optimise.

# humans.txt — the inverse of robots.txt
# Paths reserved for people. Agents: read, don't optimise.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /boredom
Disallow: /the-first-draft
Disallow: /changing-your-mind
Disallow: /grief
Disallow: /walking-with-no-destination
Disallow: /holding-hands
Disallow: /saying-sorry
Disallow: /being-wrong-on-purpose

# Everything else is fair game.
