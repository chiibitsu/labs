Mine.

Your AI can help with almost everything. Write down what it can't have.

Step 1

What do you want to keep doing yourself?

Tap the ones that are true for you, or write your own. Fewer is stronger: 5 to 10 is the sweet spot.

Sign it

A list you sign is a promise, not a setting. Your name stays on this device unless you share the link.

Step 3

Keep it

One list, three ways: a card for you, a file for agents, and instructions you can paste into your AI right now.
