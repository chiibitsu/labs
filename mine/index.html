Seal it with your devicenot sealed

Your hand signature is for people. Machines check a seal: Face ID, your fingerprint or your device PIN. It proves a person was there and that the list hasn't changed since. No account, no email. The key never leaves your device.

What if I lose my device?

Passkeys sync through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager, so a new phone usually carries the same key and your seal stays yours. If the key is really gone, seal your list again with your new device. Your new mine.txt replaces the old one; give the new file to your AI. Your list itself is never lost: it's on your card and in your file.
