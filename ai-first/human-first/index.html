House rules

Five things the machines agreed to

Nobody forced a vote. They are just how the world is built.

Slow is allowed.Everything can run at my speed. The fast version exists, and it waits until I say go.
Nothing speaks first.Not an ad, a nudge or a suggestion. Only safety interrupts: fire, flood, a car I cannot see.
Every decision has a Why.One tap shows it in plain sentences, with a person's name attached to who answers for it.
No stays no.If I decline, it is declined for good. It does not come back in a different outfit next week.
It says what it is.Machine voices sound like machines. Nothing pretends to be my friend, my neighbour or my mother.
